About Youth Empowerment

According to the original project proposal, Youth Empowerment was established in 2019 with the aim of supporting young people through education and skills development. The idea is also to give young people a positive space to develop their talents and take part in their communities.

Mission

The mission is to empower young people with useful skills and opportunities to develop their talents, build confidence and work towards meaningful opportunities.

Vision

The vision is a society where young people have equal access to quality education, skills development, employment opportunities and personal growth, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Who the website serves

Young people

Young people, particularly those between 16 and 30, can use the website to explore learning opportunities, career guidance, mentorship and ways to develop their skills.

Mentors and volunteers

Students, professionals and other volunteers can find information about contributing their time, knowledge or practical skills.

Partners and sponsors

Businesses and organisations interested in youth development can learn about the project and possible areas of support.

Keeping the website realistic

The website separates the proposed Youth Empowerment services from general youth-development information. This makes the student project more believable and avoids presenting unconfirmed partnerships or statistics as facts.
