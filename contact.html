This page gives young people, mentors, students and professionals a simple way to enquire about volunteering, partnerships or programme information.

Project references

  • Department of Basic Education: background information on youth participation and community action.

  • UNICEF South Africa: information on youth employability and pathways from learning to earning.

These sources provide context for the student website concept. They should not be read as confirmed partnerships with Youth Empowerment.
